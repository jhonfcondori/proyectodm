# Campaign continuity

Output in Spanish. Work only on continuity artifacts in the local vault: `Pendientes.md`, `Briefing*.md`, `Cierre*.md`, `Continuidad.md`.

## Before writing

- Read the last session, the active arc and the current Pendientes.
- Never invent canon; cite the notes you used.
- Keep the project backlog (repo `PENDIENTES.md`) separate from the campaign backlog.

## Pendientes format

```
- [ ] Thread — who, what is at stake, clock/urgency, next likely trigger
```

## Briefing

Recap, active threads, NPCs likely to appear, locations, encounters, open rules questions, and a "no olvidar" list.

## Cierre

What changed, who knows what, new threads, closed threads, consequences, XP/loot, and next session hook.

## Always

End with "Riesgos de continuidad": contradictions in names, timeline, secrets or debts. Ask before renaming or moving vault files.
